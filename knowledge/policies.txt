ORDER CONFIRMATION

Customer confirmation is required before an order enters preparation.

An order must contain the required customer and delivery information before confirmation.

The AI must not mark an order as CONFIRMED without explicit customer confirmation.


REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR DELIVERY ORDERS

Customer name
Phone number
Delivery address
Order items
Quantity
Required size or variant
Payment method


PAYMENT METHODS

Cash on Delivery
JazzCash
EasyPaisa


CASH ON DELIVERY

Customers can pay the rider in cash when the order is delivered.

Customers should provide the correct amount whenever possible.


JAZZCASH

JazzCash is accepted as a payment method.

Payment instructions are provided after order confirmation when applicable.


EASYPAISA

EasyPaisa is accepted as a payment method.

Payment instructions are provided after order confirmation when applicable.


ORDER MODIFICATION

Customers may request changes before the order enters preparation.

Once preparation has started, changes may not be possible.

The AI should not guarantee a modification after preparation has started.


ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers may request cancellation before preparation starts.

Once the order has entered preparation, cancellation may not be possible.

The AI should not guarantee cancellation after preparation has started.


REFUNDS

If an order is cancelled before preparation and payment has already been made, refund handling should be reviewed according to the payment method.

The AI should not invent refund amounts or processing times.

Refund cases requiring manual review should be escalated to staff.


WRONG ORDER

If a customer receives an incorrect item, the customer should contact the restaurant as soon as possible.

The order number and incorrect item should be provided.

The issue should be reviewed by restaurant staff.


MISSING ITEM

If an item is missing from an order, the customer should contact the restaurant with the order number.

The AI should collect the complaint and escalate it when necessary.


DAMAGED FOOD

If food arrives damaged or spilled, the customer should report the issue promptly.

The customer may be asked to provide a photo for verification.


FOOD ALLERGIES

Customers must inform the restaurant about known allergies before placing an order.

The restaurant cannot guarantee that food is completely free from allergens because ingredients may be prepared in a shared kitchen.


SPECIAL REQUESTS

Customers may request:

No onions
No olives
No jalapenos
Extra cheese
Extra chicken
Mild spice
Medium spice
Spicy
Extra spicy

Special requests are subject to availability.


PRICES

The AI must use the prices contained in the restaurant menu knowledge base.

The AI must not invent prices.


DISCOUNTS

Discounts and promotions may change.

The AI should only mention discounts that are available in the current business knowledge base.

The AI must not invent promotional codes.


MINIMUM ORDER

There is no minimum food order for standard delivery.

Delivery charges apply according to the delivery area unless the order qualifies for free delivery.


DUPLICATE ORDERS

If the system detects that the same customer may have submitted duplicate orders, the order should be reviewed before preparation.


CUSTOMER DATA

Customer information should only be used for order processing and delivery.

The system should not expose customer information to other customers.


AI LIMITATIONS

The AI must not:

Invent menu items
Invent prices
Invent delivery areas
Invent delivery fees
Invent discounts
Invent payment methods
Promise exact delivery times
Confirm orders without customer confirmation
Claim an order was delivered when delivery has not been confirmed


ESCALATION

The AI should escalate the conversation to a human staff member when:

A customer requests a refund requiring manual review.
A customer reports a serious complaint.
A payment problem occurs.
The delivery address is outside the listed areas.
A menu item is unclear or unavailable.
A customer disputes the charged amount.
A customer reports an allergy or food safety concern.
The system cannot confidently determine the correct action.
