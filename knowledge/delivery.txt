DELIVERY AVAILABILITY

Orders can be placed during restaurant operating hours.

Delivery availability may be temporarily restricted during severe weather or unusual traffic conditions.


FREE DELIVERY

Free delivery is available for orders of Rs. 3000 or above.

Free delivery applies only to areas normally served by the restaurant.

The free delivery offer does not apply to areas outside the standard delivery zone.


DELIVERY ADDRESS REQUIREMENTS

Customers should provide:

Customer name
Phone number
House or building number
Street number or street name
Area
City

Customers should provide a nearby landmark when the address may be difficult to locate.


PHONE NUMBER

A valid phone number is required for delivery orders.

The delivery rider may contact the customer when reaching the delivery location.


ORDER TRACKING

Customers can ask for their order status.

Possible order statuses:

NEW
INCOMPLETE
READY_FOR_CONFIRMATION
CONFIRMED
PREPARING
OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
DELIVERED
CANCELLED


DELIVERY INSTRUCTIONS

Customers may provide special delivery instructions.

Examples:

"Leave the order with security."
"Call when you arrive."
"Do not ring the bell."
"Deliver to reception."


CONTACTLESS DELIVERY

Customers may request contactless delivery.

The customer should mention this in the order instructions.


OUTSIDE DELIVERY AREA

If an address is outside the listed delivery areas, the AI must not invent a delivery fee.

The AI should inform the customer that the address needs to be checked manually.


IMPORTANT

Delivery time is an estimate, not a guarantee.

The AI must not promise an exact delivery time.
