ORDER QUESTIONS

Q: What information is required to place a delivery order?
A: The order requires the customer's name, phone number, delivery address, order items, quantities, required sizes or variants, and payment method.

Q: Do I need to confirm my order?
A: Yes. Customer confirmation is required before the order enters preparation.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Cancellation may be requested before preparation starts. Once preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible.

Q: Can I change my order?
A: Changes can be requested before preparation starts. Changes may not be possible after preparation has started.

Q: Can I add an item after placing an order?
A: You may request an addition before preparation starts. The request is subject to availability and order status.

Q: What happens if an item is missing?
A: Contact the restaurant with the order number and details of the missing item.

Q: What if I receive the wrong item?
A: Contact the restaurant as soon as possible and provide the order number and details of the incorrect item.


ORDER STATUS QUESTIONS

Q: What does NEW mean?
A: The order has been received but has not completed validation.

Q: What does INCOMPLETE mean?
A: Required order information is missing.

Q: What does READY_FOR_CONFIRMATION mean?
A: Required order information is available and the customer needs to confirm the order.

Q: What does CONFIRMED mean?
A: The customer has explicitly confirmed the order.

Q: What does PREPARING mean?
A: The restaurant has started preparing the order.

Q: What does OUT_FOR_DELIVERY mean?
A: The order has been handed to the delivery rider.

Q: What does DELIVERED mean?
A: The order has been delivered.

Q: What does CANCELLED mean?
A: The order has been cancelled.

Q: What should I do if my food arrives damaged?
A: Contact the restaurant promptly. Staff may request a photo of the damaged order.

Q: What should I do if my order is late?
A: Contact the restaurant and provide the order number so the order status can be checked.

Q: What if I entered the wrong address?
A: Contact the restaurant immediately. Address changes may not be possible after the order enters preparation or delivery.

Q: What if my address is outside the delivery area?
A: The address requires manual verification. The AI should not invent a delivery fee.
